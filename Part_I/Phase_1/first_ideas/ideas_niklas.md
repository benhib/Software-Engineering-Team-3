# Project Ideas

## Notes Exchange
Students can upload their notes and summaries and share them with other students. Other students can rate the notes, so the best ones are easy to find. This saves time, because not everyone has to write the same summary again.

## Campus Marketplace
Students can sell or give away things they don't need anymore, like used books, calculators or electronic parts. Instead of throwing things away, other students at the university can use them. This saves money and is better for the environment.

## Study Group Finder
The app uses the timetables of students to find time slots when you and your classmates are all free. It then suggests an empty room where you can study together. This makes it much easier to plan study groups.
